# Evaluations

Person 4 coordinates overall product evaluation with Person 3 and a qualified badminton coach.

Ownership:

- Person 3: `evals/pose/` and `evals/rules/`.
- Person 4: `evals/retrieval/`, `evals/generation/`, and `evals/system/`.
- Shared review: end-to-end acceptance, latency, privacy, and deletion behavior.

Participant videos must not be committed. A production rule remains disabled until it achieves at least 0.80 precision on a held-out coach-labeled set.
