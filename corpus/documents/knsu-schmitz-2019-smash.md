# Overhead smash contact

Vanessa Schmitz, Christian Bien, and Felix Weyer, "Überkopfschläge im Badminton" (2019), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation of the Smash sequence only.

The smash is an attacking shot used to force the opponent onto the defensive. It is hit steeply, just over the net, at the opponent.

Preparation matches the other overhead shots through the roof, the backswing, and the start of the loop. At contact, use an explosive pronation. The elbow extends and the racket accelerates to a maximum. The shuttle is hit clearly in front of the head and is hit downward. The follow-through is short, then a step forward.
