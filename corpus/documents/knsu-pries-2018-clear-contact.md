# Forehand overhead clear contact

Cathérine Pries and Marius Winkler, "Lernzirkel Badminton: Hoher Aufschlag / Vorhand-Überkopf-Clear" (2018), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/ui_lernzirkel_badminton_hoher_aufschlag_vorhand_ueberkopf_clear/ui_lernzirkel_badminton_hoher_aufschlag_vorhand_ueberkopf_clear.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation of the forehand overhead-clear contact cues. The high-serve station is stored separately.

The learning target for the forehand overhead clear is to hit the shuttle above the head with a nearly extended arm, and to keep the loop behind the back fluid and loose.

Stand under the shuttle and sight it with the free hand. Hit the shuttle as high above the head as possible. Before contact, with a slight bend at the elbow and wrist, inwardly rotate the shoulder and pronate the forearm while extending the wrist. The racket is accelerated strongly. The shuttle is struck at the highest point. Positioning under the shuttle matters: a clean clear depends on being in the right place.
