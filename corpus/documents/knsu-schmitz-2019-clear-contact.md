# Clear contact and arm extension

Vanessa Schmitz, Christian Bien, and Felix Weyer, "Überkopfschläge im Badminton" (2019), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. This is an English translation of the Clear sequence, split out from the drop and smash sequences in the same guide.

The clear is a freeing shot, used when the opponent's shot cannot be played offensively. A long hit to the baseline pushes the opponent back and gives the player time to recover to a better ready position.

Ready position: wait in anticipation. In the backswing, form a roof: raise the non-racket arm toward the shuttle, rotate the racket-side shoulder and the hip well back, and hold the racket at head height. In the hitting phase, raise the racket-side elbow, rotate the hip and shoulder into the hitting direction, keep a firm grip, and begin the loop.

At contact, use a strong pronation. The racket head travels over the non-racket shoulder blade until it is beside the racket side of the body. The arm is fully extended. The elbow extends quickly. The racket is strongly accelerated. The shuttle is hit above the head.
