# Overhead drop contact

Vanessa Schmitz, Christian Bien, and Felix Weyer, "Überkopfschläge im Badminton" (2019), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation of the Drop sequence only.

The drop is an attacking shot that puts the opponent under pressure and forces them out of the central position. It is played just behind the net on a moderately arched flight path.

The preparation matches the other overhead shots: ready position, roof, non-racket arm toward the shuttle, racket-side shoulder and hip rotated back, racket at head height, then the loop with the racket elbow raised. At contact, slow the hitting action. Use a soft pronation. The elbow extends and the racket accelerates only slightly. The shuttle is hit in front of the head and is hit downward. The follow-through is short, then a step forward, and the racket swings out to the non-racket side.
