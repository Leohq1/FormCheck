# High serve

Cathérine Pries and Marius Winkler, "Lernzirkel Badminton: Hoher Aufschlag / Vorhand-Überkopf-Clear" (2018), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/ui_lernzirkel_badminton_hoher_aufschlag_vorhand_ueberkopf_clear/ui_lernzirkel_badminton_hoher_aufschlag_vorhand_ueberkopf_clear.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation of the high-serve sequence. This is not an overhead clear.

Take a light split stance. Guide the racket up and back with the hitting elbow fully open at 180 degrees. The other hand holds the shuttle at the skirt with the cork down, and that arm is also fully extended.

Shift weight from the back foot to the front foot and bring the hip forward to create preload. Bring the hitting arm forward and let the racket head drop, with a slight supination of the forearm. Keep the wrist cocked. Drop the shuttle before the racket head reaches the midline of the body. Release the wrist preload and pronate the forearm quickly so the racket head accelerates. The hitting direction is forward and upward. Contact is below the waist. The follow-through travels diagonally over the non-racket shoulder. Hit from low to high, and swing the racket through to produce a high flight path. Aim high and deep into the rear of the opponent's service court.
