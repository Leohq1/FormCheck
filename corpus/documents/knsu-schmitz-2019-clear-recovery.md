# Clear follow-through and step forward

Vanessa Schmitz, Christian Bien, and Felix Weyer, "Überkopfschläge im Badminton" (2019), KNSU, Institut für Sportwissenschaft, Universität Koblenz-Landau, Campus Koblenz. https://files.knsu.de/spielsportarten/badminton/grundschlaege/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton/th_ueberkopfschlaege_im_badminton.pdf Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. English translation of the Clear follow-through only.

After the clear, take a step forward. The racket swings out to the non-racket side of the body.
